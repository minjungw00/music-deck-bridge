Control YouTube Music from Stream Deck keys and Stream Deck + dials while listening in Chrome or its installed YouTube Music app. See the current song, artist and album artwork. Control your music without switching to the player.

Features: Play/pause, previous/next, Like/Dislike, volume adjustment, mute and Open YouTube Music. Stream Deck + provides volume and track-navigation dials. Ratings require an available control in the signed-in player.

This plugin requires the companion Chrome extension on the same Windows PC:
https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ldipcjgadbppphalmibkofhigjffnlca?utm_source=elgato_marketplace&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=companion_setup

Setup: Install the extension with Add to Chrome. Add a Music Deck Bridge action and select Generate PIN in its settings. Enter the six-digit PIN in the extension popup within 10 minutes, then open music.youtube.com or the YouTube Music Chrome app.

The Chrome popup also works without Stream Deck.
Requires Windows 10/11, Chrome 116+ and Stream Deck 7.1+. Dial actions require Stream Deck +.
UI: English, Korean, Simplified/Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, French and German.
Unofficial; not affiliated with Google, YouTube, Elgato or Corsair.
