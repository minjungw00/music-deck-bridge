Control YouTube Music from Stream Deck keys and Stream Deck + dials while listening in Chrome or its installed YouTube Music app. See the song, artist and artwork. Adjust playback, volume and ratings without switching to the player.

This plugin requires the companion Chrome extension on the same Windows PC:
https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ldipcjgadbppphalmibkofhigjffnlca?utm_source=elgato_marketplace&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=companion_setup

Features: Play/pause, previous/next, mute, Like/Dislike and Open YouTube Music. Customize current-track text and artwork. Volume Up/Down keys offer 1–5 percentage-point steps and optional volume numbers, percent signs and a 0–3 second display duration (0 hides numbers). Stream Deck + adds volume and track dials. Ratings need an available signed-in player control.

Setup: Install the extension with Add to Chrome. Add a Music Deck Bridge action, select Generate PIN in its settings, then enter the six-digit PIN in the extension popup within 10 minutes. Open music.youtube.com or the YouTube Music Chrome app.

The Chrome popup also works without Stream Deck.
UI: English, Korean, Simplified/Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, French and German.
Requires Windows 10/11, Chrome 116+ and Stream Deck 7.1+. Dials require Stream Deck +. No separate Node.js/npm/Python installation.
