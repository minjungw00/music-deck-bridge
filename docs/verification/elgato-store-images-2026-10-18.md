# Elgato Marketplace image exports

Evidence date: October 18, 2026 (Asia/Seoul). The maintainer requested Marketplace icons and screenshots at the required resolutions. Five PNGs are prepared under `store-assets/elgato/listing/`: the app icon, a separate thumbnail and three numbered gallery images.

## Requirements and upload mapping

The official [product guidelines](https://docs.elgato.com/guidelines/products/) were checked on the evidence date. App icons must be 288×288 PNGs; thumbnails and gallery images must be 1920×960 PNGs with English text. At least three gallery items are required, with up to ten accepted. The thumbnail is a separate upload field. The [upload guide](../../store-assets/elgato/README.md#upload-files) maps all five files to the Console fields and gallery order.

## Source and rendering provenance

- The app icon is an unchanged copy of the existing 288px `store-assets/elgato/app-icon.png`, generated by the project's brand renderer.
- The thumbnail uses the existing 512px main mark and five action PNGs. It is a new layout sized for Marketplace, separate from the unchanged README banner.
- All three galleries reuse the approved actual **658×513** Stream Deck capture at native pixel dimensions. Gallery 1 describes playback, ratings and launch keys; gallery 2 describes the two dials; gallery 3 shows the connection to the English extension popup.
- The popup in gallery 3 is the approved stable **1.1.1**, **435×678** sample-data rendering, placed at native size and labelled accordingly. The [official source record](official-images-2026-10-18.md) distinguishes that fixture from the actual device capture. No new hardware, settings or live-service capture is inferred.
- `scripts/render-elgato-store.mjs` composes HTML/CSS in Chromium **156.0.8078.4**, device scale 1, without network requests, bridge startup or credentials. Local Segoe UI fonts supply the English text during rendering; no font files are redistributed.

## Verification

The thumbnail and all three galleries were visually inspected. PNG headers confirm exact dimensions, 8-bit channels and no interlacing. The app icon is RGBA and byte-identical to its canonical source; thumbnail/gallery PNGs are opaque RGB. Three separate gallery files are present and the explanatory text is English.

Decoded-pixel comparisons show zero differences in the actual Stream Deck UI throughout all three galleries, excluding only the 24px outer background margin affected by rounded card corners. The full popup in gallery 3 is pixel-identical to the approved rendering. Neither source image is stretched or resampled. The source capture and popup hashes remain those in the official image record.
